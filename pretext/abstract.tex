% The creation of web applications involves a series of repetitive steps and abstractions that consume time, financial resources, and effort. Additionally, standardizing the structure and best practices in these applications is challenging. To address this issue, we propose the Vortex web framework. Vortex aims to enhance productivity and performance, particularly through its Object-Relational Mapper (ORM). Its advantages over available PHP frameworks in the market were demonstrated in an experimental study. The tests provided tangible evidence of productivity improvement. Furthermore, through data acquired in a controlled and comparative environment, it was possible to conclude performance gains compared to other examined frameworks.
